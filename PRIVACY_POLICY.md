## **What the extension does**

Bring Back Google Maps Button adds a Maps button to Google Search. The button lets you open Google Maps with the same search term.

## **How the extension uses your search term**

The extension reads the search term from the current Google Search page’s URL and uses it in your browser to build a Google Maps link. When you click the Maps button, your browser opens that link over HTTPS and sends the search term to Google Maps. Google handles that request under its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## **Storage and tracking**

The extension does not save your search terms or send them to the developer. It does not use analytics or tracking, and the developer does not sell user data.
